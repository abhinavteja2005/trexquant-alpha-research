Paste this as the first message of any new Claude chat:

> This is the TrexQuant Alpha Factory. Clone or pull github.com/abhinavteja2005/trexquant-alpha-research and read, in order: CLAUDE.md (hard rules, including NO DELETIONS), handoff/MEMORY.md (LATEST section), reports/status.md (who owns the runner), reports/RESEARCH_REPORT.md (story so far), alphas/registry.md (every alpha and its status). Do not touch Chrome unless status.md says you are the runner owner. Then tell me in 5 lines where things stand.
