## Notes
- None of the drafts use a market-wide series on its own; every macro series is multiplied by, or correlated with, a stock-level return, so de-meaning does not cancel them.
- None use trading_days_until_next_earnings_announcement or negative delays. All windows and lags are allowed; every flag is cast with f = close/close.
- `2*nxt - 1` stays float32 (a Python int scalar times float32).
